# Mathematics lesson

Use for a mathematical object, formula, proof idea, or real-valued relationship.

## Verify

Prefer textbooks, papers, course notes from trusted institutions, and primary mathematical references. Independently verify formulas, domains, boundary behavior, plotted values, and examples. Use deterministic TypeScript calculations for interactive points. If a WolframAlpha tool is available, use it as an additional check, not as the only justification.

## Lesson shape

For real-valued mathematics:

1. begin with a graph or geometric question;
2. expose one or two parameters the learner can vary;
3. ask for a conjecture before naming the formula;
4. connect each symbolic step to the visible geometry;
5. include a counterexample, limiting case, or domain boundary;
6. finish with a difficult transfer or synthesis problem.

SVG and ordinary React state are usually enough. Keep the coordinate transform and mathematical calculation deterministic and tested. Label a heuristic picture `conceptual`; label a numerically faithful plot `exact` only within its recorded sampling/precision limits.
