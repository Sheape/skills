# Pedagogy

## Lesson shape

Teach one capability tied to the topic mission and just above the learner's demonstrated floor. Use the beats that help:

1. establish a useful question;
2. ask for a deterministic prediction or conjecture;
3. reveal the mechanism one meaningful change at a time;
4. give specific authored feedback;
5. test transfer on a different example;
6. expose the strongest frozen sources.

Keep prose subordinate to the mechanism. A difficult transfer task is better evidence than replaying the demonstration. Every lesson includes at least one checkable decision; open-ended discussion may follow but does not grade or unlock completion.

## Visual systems

Use progressive ERDs, architecture boundaries, request and data flows, ownership movement, and state transitions when relationships carry the explanation. Begin with the minimum structure, mute irrelevant parts, and animate stable semantic IDs. Keep each diagram step near nine visible nodes.

## Code

Authored traces need not execute. Make the active line, before or after phase, call depth, and event explicit. Separate source, variables, literal console output, conceptual call state, and data movement. Use representative cases such as typical, high-volume or many-data, empty or no-input, and one meaningful edge case without cataloguing niche curiosities.

Editable code is a scratchpad with verified expected output beside it. The learner runs it on their own machine if they want execution.

## Mathematics

For real-valued math, begin with a graph or geometric question. Let the learner vary a parameter and form a conjecture before the formula. Connect symbolic steps to visible geometric change, then include a counterexample or boundary that breaks the easy guess. Finish with difficult transfer or synthesis.

## Fidelity and records

- `exact`: matches the defined program, source, data, or mathematics.
- `simplified`: omits irrelevant detail while preserving shown relationships.
- `conceptual`: a teaching model, not a claim about literal internals.

Put the label beside the relevant state. Record learning only after evidence. Exposure, completion, and quick recognition do not prove durable understanding.
