# Pedagogy

Teach one capability tied to the topic mission. Ask for a prediction before the reveal when useful, expose one meaningful change per step, give immediate authored feedback, and finish with a transfer task that cannot be solved by copying the example.

Choose the lesson mode from the mechanism being taught, not from the topic name. A lesson may borrow another mode's visual grammar, but it has one primary mode in `meta.ts`.

Coverage is not evidence of learning. Update learning records only after the learner demonstrates understanding, corrects a misconception, states relevant prior knowledge, or changes the mission.
