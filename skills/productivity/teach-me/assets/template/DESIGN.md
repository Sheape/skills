# Design direction

## Thesis

Authored states make mechanisms inspectable. The application is a quiet technical notebook, not a one-page lesson dump or a simulated IDE.

## Visual world

Light mode borrows Notion's warm paper, white cards, quiet dividers, and blue action color. Dark mode borrows Vercel's black canvas, near-black elevated surfaces, white type, and sharp hairlines. Geist carries prose; JetBrains Mono Nerd Font Propo carries code.

## Interaction story

The learner chooses a lesson, predicts, steps through semantic state, inspects frozen citations, attempts transfer, and explicitly marks completion. Motion connects states and disappears under reduced-motion preferences.

## Constraints

One reading column sits beside a compact topic rail. All controls remain usable from the keyboard and on narrow viewports. Shared components cover only repeated teaching behavior; unusual visuals stay lesson-local until reuse is proven.
